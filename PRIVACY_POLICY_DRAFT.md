# Privacy Policy for Magic Carrom 2D

> **Draft — review before publishing.** Verify that the information below
> matches the actual release build and all enabled services.

Effective date: 27-11-2026

Magic Carrom 2D ("the game") is provided by ClassFly ("we", "us", or
"our"). This policy describes how information is handled when you use the game.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores your chosen player name, the name entered for the pass-and-play
opponent, sound preference, and whether you have seen certain gameplay guides
on your device. These are used to remember your preferences and provide the
game's features.

You can change player names and sound settings in the game. You can remove
locally stored game data using your device's app settings or by uninstalling
the game. This may also remove your preferences, and any device or cloud
backups may need to be handled separately.

## Online multiplayer

Online multiplayer uses Photon Fusion and Photon Cloud, provided by Exit Games,
to connect players and run matches. When you play online, your chosen player
name, room code, and match information are used to establish and play the
session. Your chosen player name is shared with the other player in your match.
Please avoid using sensitive information in your player name or room code.

Photon processes network information, including your device's IP address, to
provide its networking service. Photon may also process a user identifier when
one is provided by the game. For more information, see
[Photon's data privacy information](https://www.photonengine.com/terms/gdpr)
and [Photon's privacy policy](https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy).

## How information is used and shared

We use the information described above to remember settings, connect players,
and operate online matches. Multiplayer information is shared with Photon to
provide the service and with the other player as described above.

We do not currently maintain our own database of player information.
This version of the game does not contain advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device remains there until you change or remove it,
subject to any backups. We do not currently maintain our own database of player
information. Photon says it keeps personal data only for as long as necessary;
its public data privacy information does not specify a retention period for
Photon Cloud end-user data. For more information, see
[Photon's data privacy information](https://www.photonengine.com/terms/gdpr).

To ask about your information or request assistance with deletion, contact us
using the details below. We will review requests in accordance with applicable
law. Removing local data does not necessarily remove information held by a
third-party provider.
